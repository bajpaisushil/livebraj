## Sensitivity

- Rangji restricts entry. Entwistle (1987, p. 217) calls it 'the only important temple in Vrindaban that refuses admission to non-Hindus - a restriction that also applies to converts', and (p. 410) says non-Hindus are not admitted to the inner part. Hayagriva Dasa recorded in 1972: 'Foreigners are not allowed'. A public app that lets anyone walk up to the mula images crosses the temple's own boundary. Keep the player at the darshan line and consider asking the Sri Rangji Temple Trust (contact page on srirangjimandir.org) before shipping close-up sanctum models.
- Photographing the main deity or main building at Rangji is reported as prohibited (omusafir 2016 blog; vrindavanmathuraguide). A detailed 3D replica may be seen the same way, which is another reason to ask the trust.
- Never let the camera or player go inside, behind or beside a garbhagriha, clip through a murti, or touch one. Allow darshan and pranam only; the project rule is no gamified devotion.
- Show the murtis dressed at all times. Abhishekam and undressed states are private sevas.
- Respect closures and curtains. Rangji: 11:00-16:00 (Apr-Aug) or 12:00-15:00 (Sep-Mar), closing at 21:00. Katyayani, per its trust: open 07:00-12:00 with a break 11:30-12:00. Gopishwar: roughly 12:00-16:30 closed.
- Gopishwar's gopi-vesh is devotional theology: Shiva became a gopi to enter Krishna's rasa and guards it. Render it with dignity. No comic, gender-joke or sexualised treatment, and no on-screen morph.
- Katyayani's founder declared her 'Param Vaishnavi' and her 'bali' is sugarcane. Show no blood or sacrifice, and render the Mahishasura group with restraint.
- Rangji follows the Tenkalai Sri Vaishnava tradition, and its namam is a sectarian identity mark. Render it correctly or leave it off. Never substitute a Gaudiya or Vadakalai tilak.
- Aniconic sacred objects (the Narsingh shila, the engraved yantra, the lingas) are not decoration. If their form is unknown, omit them rather than inventing one.
- Guru murtis such as Keshavananda and Satyananda depict real people. Model them only from documented images of the murtis themselves, or omit them.
- Use the temples' own names: Sri Goda-Rangamannar, Sri Katyayani Devi, Sri Gopishwar Mahadev.

## Do not model

- Do not make Rangji's principal deity a reclining Ranganatha on Shesha. The trust itself says Ranganatha is presented here 'as Rangamannar (Dulha Raja) in a standing pose along with Andal (Goda) and Garudji as the Principal Deity', and the 1968 plate and the 2026 photo agree. altars.js marks this as contested and provisional; it is now resolved.
- Do not use the tan SKIN colour for Rangji's mula images. The visible faces measure near-black: Rangamannar about #0c141f, Srinivasa about #161817, the Paudanath shrine figures about #1c2329.
- Do not give Rangamannar a flute, a peacock feather, a North-Indian tribhanga, a Braj mukut, or four arms with conch and discus. He stands frontally under a tall South-Indian kireeta. In the 1968 plate his right hand is raised holding a curved object and his left hand holds the staff.
- Do not make Andal and Garuda nearly as tall as Rangamannar; the 1968 plate puts them at about 0.65-0.75 of his height. Do not give Garuda a bare head: he is crowned, with spread wings and hands in anjali.
- Do not copy Srivilliputhur, Srirangam, Pushkar's Rangji or the Edulabad Goda-Rangamannar temple onto Vrindavan. For example, Andal's parrot in the left hand is not documented here. File:Aandal Rangamannar.jpg is Srivilliputhur.
- Do not put the mula images on vahanas, the rath or the Sheesh Mahal swing. Only the small utsava murtis go out.
- Do not model the Vahan Ghar exhibits as worshipped murtis: Garuda, Hanuman, Sesha, Simha, Simha-Shardul, Gaja-Vyala, elephant, horse, swan, parrot, sun and moon halos, Purnakothi and the palanquins. The same goes for the Leela Ghar's electrically operated dolls. They are processional mounts and tableaux in museum rooms.
- Do not invent forms for Rangji's Narasimha, Sudarshan, Venugopal, Alwar, Acharya, Raghunathji, Hanuman or Jaya-Vijaya images, because none is documented. Use a plainly dressed generic figure or a closed shrine door.
- Do not model the Paudanath/Ranganath shrine's posture (reclining or upright) until it is verified.
- Katyayani: do not seat her on a lotus and do not make her black stone. The trust documents a one-piece ashta-dhatu casting of 'Ma, vahan and Asur' (lion and buffalo). In altars.js, 'devi-seated' with 8 arms and 'contested material' should become: material SOURCED, lotus unsupported, arm count UNKNOWN, lion and asura present.
- Katyayani: do not use ASHTA_DHATU 0xa8823c (a bright bronze-gold) for her face. It measures about #42301c, with highlights about #9f7d56.
- Katyayani: do not add ear-rings. The founder's 1923 instruction was 'the deity would have no ear-rings'.
- Katyayani: do not fix her arms at 8 (unsourced) or at 4. The Hindi press's four arms with abhaya, varada, sword and lotus is a generic Navadurga description; it does not match the trident, curved blade and shield in the photo.
- Katyayani: do not show the 1923 'England-returned' small Ganesh as today's murti. A Makrana-marble Ganesh replaced it on 30 Nov 2020.
- Katyayani: do not treat the gate lions or the trust's logo (the Samhara Yantra on a lotus) as murtis.
- Katyayani: do not invent the 'Uchchwal Chandrahas' sword. Only guide sites report it; the trust pages read do not.
- Gopishwar: do not model a bare stone linga with an invented size, stone or shape, because none is documented. Model the documented covered states.
- Gopishwar: do not use File:Gopeswar Mahadev.jpg. It shows a standing painted statue of Shiva as a gopi with bilingual plaques, somewhere other than this temple.
- Gopishwar: do not animate the change from linga to gopi in front of the player. Switch states while the shrine is closed, roughly 12:00-16:30.
- Gopishwar: do not invent Entwistle's 'old sculptures' in the circumambulation gallery, or exact placements for Parvati, Ganesh, Nandi and Annapurna.
- Do not use temple-website or Facebook darshan photos as textures or tracing references; they are copyrighted. The trust gallery images viewed during this research were used only to cross-check.
- Do not use File:Maa Katyayani.jpg (it is in Mathura, not Vrindavan), File:Santh Maharaj.jpg (location unidentified) or File:Raasleela of Shri Gopeshwar Mahadev.jpg (a stage performance).

## The murtis, one by one

### Sri Rangamannar

**temple:** Shri Ranganath (Rangaji / Sri Rangji) Mandir, Vrindavan

**name:** Sri Rangamannar: the principal mula-vigraha of the Sri Goda-Rangamannar sannidhi (Ranganatha/Krishna as the bridegroom)

**posture:** STANDING and frontal. He is NOT reclining. SOURCED from the temple trust's Sri Dhanurmasam page: Rangadeshika Swami envisaged 'Lord Ranganatha (Lord Vishnu- with reclining pose) as Rangamannar (Dulha Raja) in a standing pose along with Andal (Goda) and Garudji as the Principal Deity'. The trust's history page (EN and HI) adds: 'Lord Krishna is present as the bridegroom with a walking stick in his hand'. MEASURED from two photographs: he stands in the plate 'Bhagwan Sri Ranganath ji' in P. D. Mital 1968 (plate listed at p. 570), and also in the Commons photo of the sanctum taken 19 Apr 2026. Guide sites that put a reclining Ranganatha on Shesha in the main sanctum are conflating it with Srirangam or with the separate Paudanathji shrine (see that entry).

**height:** No figure in metres: UNKNOWN. SOURCED in words: Mital 1966 (p. 229-230) says the main temple holds 'विशाल' (huge) images of Ranganath, 'Lakshmi' and Garuda, and that no other Vrindavan temple has deity images this large. MEASURED from both photos: his crown is about 1.5-2 times the height of his face. ESTIMATED: he is the tallest of the three sanctum figures. Goda and Garuda reach about 0.65-0.75 of his height.

**material:** UNKNOWN. The trust does not state it. SOURCED from Mital 1968 p. 572: all of the temple's images were made in the South by South-Indian image-makers and brought from there at great cost. MEASURED: the face and arms show a glossy near-black surface in the 2026 photo. ESTIMATED: a dark South-Indian-type stone.

**murtiColour:** Near-black. MEASURED median of the face below the namam is about #0c141f, from File:Interior view of Sri Rangji Temple Vrindavan showing sanctum and chandelier.jpg (CC BY-SA 3.0). The reading is uncalibrated and the bluish cast comes from the lighting. Do not use the project's tan SKIN colour.

**attributes:**
- Tall conical or cylindrical gold kireeta (crown) with a small finial. MEASURED in both the 1968 plate and the 2026 photo.
- White Sri Vaishnava namam on the forehead. MEASURED as a white band. The temple is Tenkalai (SOURCED, Entwistle 1987 p. 410). The exact outline cannot be resolved at this photo distance.
- Right hand RAISED to shoulder height with the forearm vertical, holding a large curved, crescent-like object. MEASURED from the 1968 plate. The 2026 photo shows a raised dark hand holding a pale object on the viewer's left. What the object is: UNKNOWN.
- Left hand LOWERED at the hip, holding a thin staff with an ornamented head. MEASURED from the 1968 plate. This matches the trust's 'walking stick in his hand as is the custom in a traditional south Indian marriage' (SOURCED), but the trust does not say which hand.
- Only two arms are visible in the 1968 plate, with no conch or discus. MEASURED from a halftone, so medium confidence.
- Pale flower ornaments at ear level on both sides of the face. MEASURED in 2026; probably changing alankara.
- No flute, peacock feather, tribhanga or Braj-style mukut is documented for this form.

**arrangement:** SOURCED from the trust (EN and HI): Goda Devi (Andal) stands to his own RIGHT, which is the viewer's left. Garuda stands to his own LEFT, the viewer's right. All three are mula images in one sanctum. MEASURED from the 1968 plate: the same order. The two flanking figures are clearly shorter, with crown tops at about 0.65-0.75 of his height (ESTIMATED, because their bases are hidden). The utsava murtis stand on a platform in front. The project's flanking heights of 1.26 and 1.30 against 1.52 are too tall.

**altarSetting:** MEASURED from the 2026 photo: the sanctum doorway sits in a gilded carved frame hung with rows of brass bells. Behind the figures is a pale lilac/white cloth backdrop under a dark navy-and-silver arch, with a small name board (yellow border, blue field) above the crown. In front is a white/silver balustraded platform crowded with small utsava murtis in white, pink and red, and a brass lamp stand stands at the front. The approach is a mandapa with red-and-green painted pillars carrying relief figures, a crystal chandelier and a floor kolam. SOURCED from the trust: the sannidhi is at the centre of the five concentric enclosures and 'Sri Jaya-Vijaya' guard its entrance. No curtain detail is published.

**fixedVsChanging:** FIXED: the image, its standing pose and the raised-right/lowered-left arms. These are the same in the 1968 plate and the 2026 photo, and no record of a replacement was found. CHANGING: the vastra and garlands. On 19 Apr 2026 at 16:30 he wore a white garment with long red-and-gold zari borders and heavy white garlands (MEASURED). DAILY, SOURCED from the trust's darshan page: Mangala aarti and Vishwaroop darshan 05:30; Divya aradhan 06:30-07:30; darshan closed 11:00-16:00 (Apr-Aug) or 12:00-15:00 (Sep-Mar); night aradhan 19:30; darshan closes 21:00. PERIODIC, SOURCED from the trust's seva and utsav pages: Panchaparva abhishekam (1st of the month, Ekadasi, Amavasya, Purnima and birth-stars); Phool Bangla in peak summer; Dhanurmasa 16 Dec-14 Jan, with the Goda-Rangamannar wedding on its last day; Pavitrotsava in Aug/Sep; Vaikuntha Ekadashi. The mula image never goes out. All processions use the utsava murtis.

- Andal's hair and crown form and the object in her raised hand are UNKNOWN. So is Garuda's lower body: standing is MEASURED to the chest only.
- The figure at the right edge of the 2026 sanctum photo is only partly visible. Identifying it as Garuda is ESTIMATED.
- Paudanath shrine: its identity with File:Sri Rangnath Ji Maharaj.jpg is ESTIMATED, from the timestamps (19 minutes after the Katyayani photo next door), the Sri Vaishnava dvarapalas and a visitor blog's signboard. Its posture is unresolved. Whether Mahalakshmi is a separate image, and which consort stands where, are UNKNOWN.
- Raghunathji as Rama with Sita and Lakshmana is reported only by secondary sources. The forms of Sudarshan, Narasimha, Venugopal, the Alwars, the Acharyas, Hanuman and Jaya-Vijaya are all UNKNOWN.
- Srinivasa: the mudras of the lower hands, his height, and the meaning of the trust's asterisk are UNKNOWN.
- Rangji dates: the trust gives 1845-1851. Mital 1968 says construction began in Samvat 1901 and was completed in Samvat 1912 (about 1855), with the gift deed dated 18 Mar 1857. The rath is 50 ft on the trust's history page and 60 ft on its Brahmotsava page.
- Whether non-Hindus are still kept out of Rangji's inner courts in 2026: UNKNOWN. The sources are 1972 and 1987.
- Katyayani: seated or standing; the arm count; which hand holds which weapon; whether the silver weapons are cast or separate; which dark lower form is the lion and which the buffalo; and her height (the estimate is LOW confidence against the press's 'विशाल'). All UNKNOWN.

- Katyayani's Chandrashekhar Shiva: linga or figure, and five-faced or not, are UNKNOWN. His material is contested: five-metal from Calcutta (memoir) or made in Jaipur (trust page). He is also called 'Bhotesh' and 'Panchanan'.
